When you contact Neighborhood PC Repair, you deal directly with me. I bring my Windows and desktop support experience to practical problems such as slow computers, printer issues, upgrades and setting up replacement PCs.

Support for home and work.

I help local home users and small businesses that need occasional computer support. On-site visits and remote support are available by appointment, depending on the issue and location.
