How I can help.

Windows PC troubleshooting

Help with slow startup, freezing, error messages, software problems and unwanted programs. I’ll assess the issue and explain the options before you decide how to proceed.

RAM & SSD upgrades

Compatibility advice and installation where an upgrade makes sense for your device and budget. Any parts and installation costs are discussed before work proceeds.

Printer & Wi-Fi support

Printer installation, connection troubleshooting and everyday Wi-Fi issues for homes and small offices. Tell me which devices are affected and what has changed.

New computer setup & file transfer

Set up a new Windows computer, connect peripherals and transfer accessible files from your old device. Transfer options depend on the condition and accessibility of the old storage.

Small-business computer support

Occasional support for office PCs, printers and everyday software issues, without an ongoing support contract. On-site and remote appointments are arranged around availability.

File recovery assessment

Help retrieving photos and documents from accessible drives, including some computers that won’t start. Recovery is not guaranteed. Physically damaged drives may require a specialist recovery service.

Local business support

Practical help for a small office.

For owner-operated businesses and small teams that need help with a specific computer or printer issue.

  • Troubleshoot a slow or unreliable Windows PC
  • Set up a replacement computer and transfer accessible files
  • Connect a printer or troubleshoot device connectivity
  • Assess whether a PC upgrade is worthwhile
